**Company:** [Name] | **Effective:** [Date] | **Applies to:** employees, contractors and anyone using company accounts or devices

## Accounts and passwords

- Use a unique password for each work account, stored in the company password manager, [tool].
- Keep two-step verification on for every account that offers it.
- Never share accounts or approve a sign-in prompt you did not start.

## Devices

- Keep automatic updates on and the screen lock set to [5] minutes or less.
- Encrypt laptops and phones that hold company data.
- Report a lost or stolen device to [contact] within one hour.

## Email and messages

- Treat unexpected attachments, links and urgent requests with suspicion.
- Confirm any change to payment details by calling a number you already have, never one from the message.

## Company data

- Store company data only in approved services: [list].
- Do not send company data to personal email or personal cloud accounts.
- Do not enter confidential or personal information into AI tools unless [Role] has approved the tool for that use.
- Share files with named people instead of "anyone with the link".

## Software

Install only software approved by [Role]. Ask before signing up for a new service that will hold company data.

## Monitoring

The company may review activity on company systems to protect them, as allowed by law. [Have this section reviewed for your province and workforce.]

## Breaking this policy

Report suspected incidents to [contact] right away. Good-faith reports are never punished. Deliberate violations may lead to discipline, up to ending employment or a contract.
