| Question | Answer |

| Will they hold customer or employee personal information? | Yes / No |

| Will they connect to our systems or have our passwords? | Yes / No |

If every answer is No, the first section below is enough. If any answer is Yes, complete the whole checklist.

## Basics

- [ ] The company and its contact details are real and verifiable
- [ ] Two-step verification is available on our accounts
- [ ] They publish a privacy policy that covers our use

## Data handling

- [ ] We know what data they will hold and why they need it
- [ ] We know the country where it is stored
- [ ] Data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- [ ] We can export our data and have it deleted when we leave

## Security practice

- [ ] They hold a current SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 report, or answered our questions in writing
- [ ] Access to our data is limited to staff who need it
- [ ] They make regular backups and test restoring them

## Contract

- [ ] They must tell us about a breach within [72] hours
- [ ] Liability and data ownership are stated
- [ ] They disclose subcontractors who handle our data
