**Person:** [Name] | **Role:** [Role] | **Manager:** [Name]

## Before the first day

- [ ] List the systems this role needs, and nothing more
- [ ] Create accounts with the lowest access that does the job
- [ ] Prepare an encrypted, updated device

## First day

- [ ] Set up the password manager and two-step verification
- [ ] Read and sign the acceptable use policy
- [ ] Show how to report something suspicious
- [ ] Walk through a real phishing example

## First month

- [ ] Complete security awareness training
- [ ] Confirm access still matches the role

## On a role change

- [ ] Remove access the new role does not need
- [ ] Add only what the new role requires

## When someone leaves

Do these at the moment employment ends. For a sudden departure, do them during the conversation.

- [ ] Suspend the main account and sign out all sessions
- [ ] Remove the account from admin roles, shared mailboxes and groups
- [ ] Remove mail forwarding and app passwords
- [ ] Revoke access to third-party apps, including those outside single sign-on
- [ ] Change any shared passwords the person knew
- [ ] Revoke API keys and tokens they created
- [ ] Update payment approvers and bank access
- [ ] Transfer ownership of files and documents
- [ ] Collect and wipe devices, keys and access cards
- [ ] Remove their phone and email from account recovery options
- [ ] Set a mail auto-reply, and keep records as your retention rules require
