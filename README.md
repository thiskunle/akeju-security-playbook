# Small Business Security Playbook

**Written by Adekunle Akeju, founder of GramByte Technologies Inc.**

Practical security templates for businesses without a security team. Copy them, fill in the parts in `[brackets]`, and review them once a year.

| Template | Use it to |
|---|---|
| [Incident response plan](templates/incident-response-plan.md) | Know who does what when something goes wrong |
| [Acceptable use policy](templates/acceptable-use-policy.md) | Set clear rules for company accounts and devices |
| [Vendor security checklist](templates/vendor-security-checklist.md) | Check a supplier before giving it your data |
| [Staff onboarding and offboarding](templates/staff-onboarding-offboarding.md) | Grant access properly and remove it completely |
| [Backup and recovery plan](templates/backup-and-recovery-plan.md) | Make sure you can actually restore your data |

## Where to start

If you only have one afternoon: turn on two-step verification for every account, confirm your backups restore, and fill in the contact list in the incident response plan.

## Before you adopt these

These are general templates, not legal advice. Have a qualified professional review them against your contracts and the laws that apply to you. Canadian privacy references reflect PIPEDA; provinces and sectors can have additional rules.
